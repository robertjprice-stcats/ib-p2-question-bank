!
Curriculum change, May 2021. The IB introduced a revised Language & Literature syllabus with first examinations in May 2021. From that point onward, Paper 2 has been a single comparative-essay paper of four questions shared by SL and HL (1h 45m, 30 marks). Before May 2021, the paper offered six questions with separate but identical question sets. Wordings here have been condensed for indexing; consult an official IB paper for verbatim phrasing.
